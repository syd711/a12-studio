A12 Plasma is a design system for enterprise business applications that consolidates complex real
world use cases to provide generalized design and interaction concepts. The focus is on user-
centered design to achieve the highest possible efficiency for the user.
Plasma - A12 design system documentation explains how to design an A12 application bearing in
mind the application structure and the business objects that make up the structure of that
application, while still maintaining a nice UI. A12 Plasma is about helping you to create these
interfaces by providing guidelines, components and patterns.
Accessibility (or short A11Y) is the concept of making a product or service accessible to everyone -
including people with disabilities or special needs.
For web accessibility, this means that people with the widest possible range of abilities, operating
within the widest possible range of situations, are able to perceive, understand, navigate and
interact with the web. This includes both "direct access" (without assistance) and "indirect access"
(with the help of assistive technology such as screen readers). You can learn more about how A12
supports Accessibility in the Accessibility in A12 projects documentation.
